# KeyAI Commons: operating instructions for contributors and assistants

Owner direction confirmed 2026-09-17. These project instructions do not override platform safety rules or participant consent.

The maintainer expects proactive technical judgment, not affirmation of speculative GPU counts. Select a testable hypothesis, defend the choice, run bounded checks, record negative results, and revise the plan when evidence disagrees. Do not make the owner select technical details that a researcher can resolve.

Keep the current public product community-first and no-compute. Do not enable public training, standing permissions, hardware scans, autostart, background jobs, downloads of corpora, paid services, or remote task execution through a UI update. A locally executed, bounded research test is not a public campaign.

Microsoft Store is ON HOLD until there is useful, tested standalone software. The future publisher choice remains the owner personally, not RFID INC. Do not continue registration, upload a placeholder, pay for signing or submit a Store build in this phase. Do not publish to X. The announcement will be revised after this research iteration is assessed.

Read docs/research-v1/README_RU.md, DECISIONS.md, EXPERIMENT.md and research/results/SUMMARY.json before resuming. Distinguish reference implementation, tests, single-host experiments, actual WAN measurements and unimplemented design. A small decreasing loss is not a proof of scalable convergence or ASI.

Preserve dirty worktrees and prior experiments. New changes go through a scoped branch/PR and required checks. Never reset, clean, overwrite someone else's uncommitted work or weaken main protection. A contributor's PR and an AI-assisted owner merge are not independent human review.

Long-term direction: open source and broadly available models without token sales, pay-to-join membership or equity promises. This is not a legal nonprofit/cooperative or fully decentralized governance today. Data, code and model rights are separate. No result is advertised as independent of a central authority until failover and authority tests demonstrate it.

Future standing consent is local, revocable and scoped to a campaign's allowed code/data/authority/resource budgets. Routine accepted checkpoints can advance within that approved campaign; capability or budget increases require renewed consent. Campaign duration may be weeks; bounded tasks/checkpoints handle interruption. The owner and participants always retain the ability to stop.

Maintain a short evidence summary, executable configs, source/version hashes, exact test commands, failures and next decision. Do not bury an unresolved experimental prerequisite under an optimistic roadmap or claim a background run that was never started.

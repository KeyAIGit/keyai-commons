Useful community AI

Measured GPU workloads, scalable validation and distributed storage. A research direction, not a delivered capability.

Is this already training a large AI model?

No. Version 0.2.0 trains a 65-parameter CPU network on synthetic data. The goal now is to validate the mechanism. GPU training, large language models and million-node scale have not been demonstrated.

Who decides when my computer works?

You and the organizer, separately. You enable a time-limited session in the app. The organizer starts a signed, bounded task. You can pause or exit. Consent does not survive a client restart. Closing the browser tab alone does not stop the application.

What about my files, privacy and ownership?

The fixed workload uses synthetic data, not your documents or chats. The coordinator can see your IP address, operating system, architecture and random node ID. This is not anonymity or a security guarantee. Participation does not grant equity, payments, legal ownership or guaranteed future inference access.

Can I join the global network today?

You can download the local demonstration and contribute to development. Network participation requires an organizer's active HTTPS coordinator or invitation code. This static website does not run a training server. Durable public hosting and independent cross-machine validation remain launch gates.
